# Growth Audit — Hevy

**Live page: https://plykhvar-glitch.github.io/hevy-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the athletes Hevy already has, plus a clickable prototype
of the onboarding and Pro paywall rebuilt in Hevy's own design: the welcome carousel, units, the
welcome tour, the HEVY PRO paywall, an offer on close, sign-up and the Apple Health and
notification asks. Pick lbs and the tour follows. English, Spanish and Portuguese, light and
Hevy's own dark mode, a 30-second walkthrough, and a QR code to try it on a phone.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Today | In the prototype | Expected impact |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | Catch "Not now" with the $2.99 monthly plan | Not now and Skip close the paywall | A sheet offers the monthly plan with Hevy's own Free vs Pro table | ARPU +10–15% |
| 2 | Let them in before the sign-up | Sign up with Apple, Google or Email is step two | The carousel ends in Get started; the account comes after the paywall | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 3 | Show the tour before asking for Pro | The welcome tour plays after the paywall | The tour comes first, in the units they picked | CR +10–20% |
| 4 | Ask for Health and notifications after the paywall | Two system dialogs before the price | Both after the paywall, on Hevy's Apple Health screen | CR +10–15% |
| 5 | Put Apps We Love and 630,000 ratings above the plans | Proof sits below the button | Featured by Apple and 4.9 from 630,000+ ratings right above the plans | CR +10–15% |

*Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps —
not measured lift for Hevy.*

Prices, plans, the rating, the athlete count, the review, the feature rows and the Apps We Love
badge come from Hevy's own in-app screens, hevyapp.com and its App Store listing.
